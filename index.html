The work

Saturated colour on canvas, dense line on paper.

I work in acrylic on canvas and in black ink on paper. The paintings lean toward strong colour: sea blues, sunset oranges, flamingo pink, peacock green. The drawings go the other way, built from pattern, fine line and shading.

Subjects come from places I have seen and things I like to look at for a long time: a house on a hillside, a car on a mountain road, a bird in the last light of the day.
